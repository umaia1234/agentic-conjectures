# ETP Problem 8.1: Equation 677 and Equation 255 in finite magmas

Equation 677 is $x=y\diamond(x\diamond((y\diamond x)\diamond y))$ and
Equation 255 is $x=((x\diamond x)\diamond x)\diamond x$. The Equational
Theories Project (ETP) showed that 677 does not imply 255 for arbitrary
magmas; the finite case is Problem 8.1 of
[Tao et al., *The Equational Theories Project*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07087).
The FormalConjectures statement ([upstream snapshot](upstream/README.md)) asks:

> “Does Equation 677 imply Equation 255 in every finite magma?”

Its docstring adds that the project tentatively conjectures the answer is no,
i.e. that a finite countermodel exists. **The problem remains open.** This
directory records a partial result: certified and exhaustive lower bounds on
the size of a countermodel, a search of one structured family, and the Lean
facts behind them. No novelty is claimed.

## Results

A *countermodel* is a finite magma satisfying E677 but not E255.

1. **Lean** ([`Etp677Implies255Finite.lean`](../../AgenticConjectures/Etp677Implies255Finite.lean),
   no `sorry`, no extra axioms, no `native_decide`): the upstream statement as
   `def statement : Prop`; the structure facts behind every search rule
   (bijective left translations, fixer uniqueness, inverse forms of E677,
   consequences of E255 failing at a point); the reduction to countermodels
   generated by one element; and the translation-invariant ansatz with the
   soundness of its CNF and of its automorphism symmetry breaking.
2. **No countermodel has at most 10 elements (DRAT-certified).** For each
   $n\le10$, the CNF stating "there is an E677 magma on $\{0,\dots,n-1\}$
   generated by $0$, in canonical labelling, with E255 failing at $0$" is
   unsatisfiable, and `drat-trim` verifies a DRAT proof of this. The proofs
   for $n\le8$ are committed and checked in CI; those for $n=9,10$ are
   regenerated by the command below.
3. **The same bound by a second method (exhaustive search, no
   certificate).** A DFS that shares no code with the SAT encoding finds no
   countermodel with at most 10 elements. Sizes $n\le9$ are re-run in CI and
   $n=10$ was run locally (table below). For $n=11$ the DFS finished 24 of
   its 27 subtrees without a countermodel; the other 3 were stopped
   unfinished, so this directory does **not** cover $n=11$ (earlier searches
   do, see below).
4. **No translation-invariant countermodel over a group of order at most
   29 (SAT, no certificate).** For every group $G$ of order
   $\le29$ in GAP's SmallGroups library there is no fixed-point-free
   $f:G\to G$ for which $x\diamond y=x\,f(x^{-1}y)$ satisfies E677.

In general mode (E255 not negated) the same searches count the pointed E677
magmas generated by one element: for sizes $1,\dots,9$ there are
$1,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,1$ canonical tables, listed in
[`results/general_tables.txt`](results/general_tables.txt); DFS and SAT
enumeration agree.

The mathematics of every clause and rule, and what each result trusts, is in
the [mathematical details](DETAILS.md).

## Relation to earlier work

All of the following is from the ETP Zulip thread
[FINITE: 677 -> 255](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/458659-Equational/topic/FINITE.3A.20677.20-.3E.20255)
and the [blueprint chapter on Equation 677](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/677-chapter.html),
checked 2026-10-24.

- **Size bounds.** A Mace4 search excluded countermodels through size 11
  (reported 2025-03-12, 57 days for size 11), and a later DPLL model search
  (the `eq677` project) was reported to clear size 11 in about 3 hours.
  CaDiCaL runs on sizes 12–16 had not finished after 244 days. So our bound
  $n\le10$ is **not new**, and the earlier searches reach $n=11$. What this
  directory adds is a DRAT certificate for $n\le10$ (the thread proposes
  unsat certificates as the way to formalize such non-existence results; we
  found none posted) and an encoding that makes $n=10$ a single-thread SAT
  run of a few minutes.
- **Translation-invariant models.** Searches over explicit groups reported no
  fixed-point-free solution for any group of order at most 26, restricted
  searches (for example with $f^2=1$ or $f^4=1$) went further, and the
  [`eq677`](https://github.com/memoryleak47/eq677) model database was built
  with, among others, a translation-invariant search over $\mathbb Z/n$ up to
  $n=37$. The unrestricted search here starts again at order 1 and continues
  to order 29; only the seven non-cyclic groups of orders 27 and 28 go beyond
  what we found reported.
- The reduction to one-generated countermodels, the facts F1–F6 and the
  translation-invariant reformulation are standard in the thread and the
  blueprint; here they are formalized in Lean and used to justify the
  encodings.

## Exhaustive search runs

| $n$ | SAT (CaDiCaL 3.0.1) | DRAT check (drat-trim) | DFS nodes | DFS time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤8 | < 0.3 s | < 0.3 s, committed proofs | ≤ 13,705 | < 0.1 s |
| 9 | 5.2 s | 3.6 s (12.7 MB binary proof) | 831,060 | 1.3 s |
| 10 | 474.6 s | 421.9 s (631 MB binary proof) | 179,952,881 | 294 s |
| 11 | — | — | 25,714,117,407 in 24 of 27 subtrees (3 stopped) | 13.5 h CPU (finished parts) |

DFS times are single-thread on an Apple-silicon laptop. For $n=11$ the tree
was split into 300 parts at depth 7 (part 1 further into 40 sub-parts at
depth 12) and run four at a time; only parts 0–26 contain a node at depth 7,
the others just re-walk the 49 shared top nodes. Parts 20, 25 and 26 were
stopped after 4.8, 6.3 and 9.5 CPU hours, when the compute budget ran out, and
report nothing; per-part results are in
[`results/dfs_n11.txt`](results/dfs_n11.txt).

The translation-invariant search split the 88 groups of order at most 29
into 2,417 symmetry-breaking cases, all UNSAT, using 4.4 h of solver time
(one or two cases at a time, 600 s limit). The two order-28 cases that hit
the limit were re-run with limit 3600 s and took 1,575 s and 2,227 s. Order
30 was stopped after 61 of its 283 cases (one more timeout, no model) and
order 31 was not started; per-group results are in
[`results/tinv_groups.txt`](results/tinv_groups.txt).

## Reproduction

From this directory:

```bash
python3 verify_drat.py
```

regenerates the CNFs for $n\le8$ with `search/gen_cnf.py`, checks their
SHA-256 against [`certificates/SHA256SUMS`](certificates/SHA256SUMS) and runs
`drat-trim` on the committed proofs (CI, under a second). With CaDiCaL
installed,

```bash
python3 verify_drat.py --solve 9 10
```

re-solves and checks $n=9,10$ (about 16 minutes and 0.7 GB of temporary
disk).

```bash
python3 verify_dfs.py
```

compiles the DFS and re-runs $n\le9$ in both modes (CI, a few seconds),
re-checking the listed general-mode tables in Python. The $n=10,11$ runs are

```bash
g++ -O2 -std=c++17 search/dfs677.cpp -o build/dfs677
build/dfs677 10 sel 2
build/dfs677 11 sel 2 split 300 P depth 7
```

for $P=0,\dots,299$, with part 1 further split as
`split 300 1 depth 7 split2 40 Q depth2 12` for $Q=0,\dots,39$. The
translation-invariant search (needs CaDiCaL and the `passagemath-gap` Python
package for GAP) is

```bash
python3 tinv/export_groups.py 1 29 > groups.txt
python3 tinv/export_cases.py groups.txt 1 29 > cases.txt
echo 2 > workers
python3 tinv/run_cases.py groups.txt cases.txt 600 tinv.log workers
```

(600 s per case; a case that hits the limit is logged as `UNKNOWN` and was
re-run with limit 3600 s from its own cases file and log), and
`python3 tinv/test_sb.py groups.txt general_cases.txt 1 19` (with
`general_cases.txt` from `export_cases.py ... general`) validates the
symmetry breaking.

| `noCountermodelUpTo`, `statement_iff_forall_noCountermodelUpTo` | the bounded proposition certified by the searches; `statement` holds iff it holds for every bound |
| `leftMul_bijective`, `inv_form`, `inv_form₂`, `backward_recurrence` | in a finite E677 magma every left translation is a bijection; inverse forms of E677 used for propagation |
| `fixer_unique`, `eq255_at_iff_exists_fixer`, `fixer_free_of_not255`, `ne_of_not255_v`, `ne_of_not255_vii` | E255 holds at $x$ iff $x$ has a (unique) fixer, and the inequalities that hold where E255 fails |

| `tinv_translate`, `tinv_eq677_iff`, `tinv_fixer_iff`, `tinv_eq255_iff` | for $x\diamond y=x\,f(x^{-1}y)$: E677 is one functional equation for $f$, and E255 holds iff $f$ has a fixed point |

## Statement faithfulness

`Magma`, `◇`, `Equation255` and `Equation677` are copied term for term from
the upstream file, and `statement` is exactly the proposition of
`Finite.Equation677_implies_Equation255` (same `Type` universe, `Finite`
hypothesis, and quantifier order). There is no indexing or subtraction to
diverge on. `noCountermodelUpTo N` is the bounded version certified by the
searches; it is a definition only, since the bound rests on DRAT and search
results rather than on Lean.
