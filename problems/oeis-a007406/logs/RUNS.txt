Notes
  - Sections 1 and 2 are the files logs/roots_R1_2642245.txt and
    logs/roots_R2_2642245.txt; a "time" block follows each one here.
  - In R2 output, "v" is always printed as 2 and means ">= 2" (R2 works
    mod q^2); R1 works mod q^3 and reports roots_v>=3 = 0.
  - "roots_v>=1(m<q-1)" counts the roots 1 <= m <= q-2 with q | H2(m);
    "wolstenholme_congruence_failures" counts primes with q not dividing
    H2(q-1) (expected 0 for q >= 5).
  - The trailing "0" in section 6 is the return value of check() (the
    number of mismatches), echoed by gp.
