## What the certificate checks

- It compares the official OEIS initial terms against values generated by
  the original periodic-coefficient recurrence.
- In the six cases of \(t\bmod6\), it confirms that the linear coefficient
  of the three-step transition is 6 in every case, and that the constant
  terms are exactly \((13,9,7,17,6,8)\).
- For \(1\le t<1000\), it checks that the three-step transition is unchanged
  when shifted by 6.
- For all \(10\le n\le10000\), it checks
  \(a_n=6a_{n-3}+a_{n-6}-6a_{n-9}\).
